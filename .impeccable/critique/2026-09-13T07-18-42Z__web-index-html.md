## Design Specificity Verdict
Authored for this product: round 368 px stage on panel black, round thumbnails, hardware-fed gauge, Send labels that count packet types, "it never confirms" copy. Generic edges: README-style tagline, bare `<pre>` drawer, empty stage as a black hole.
Deterministic scan (degraded CLI + in-page): `layout-transition` (meter fill; false positive), `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` on div.bar (false positive, negative spread), `text-overflow` on button.send-each (measurement artefact), `flat-type-hierarchy` 12/14/16/21.8 px. Detector-only catches: mobile overflow 9 px clipping the readout; drawer sub-label contrast 4.12:1; /VERSION 404 locally.

## Priority Issues
1. [P0] Send has no consequence framing — irreversible, unacknowledged, appends to flash; override checkbox beside it. Fix: consequence line under the verbs, override moved near the gauge and styled as danger, "Send anyway" label when ticked. `/impeccable harden`
2. [P1] Drag on the stage is a silent no-op at zoom 1 and leaks a page text selection. Fix: preventDefault on pointerdown in .drop; transient "zoom in to reposition" hint or drag-to-zoom at zoom 1. `/impeccable polish`
3. [P1] Keyboard/screen-reader path half built: ring has no role or keyboard pan, rows are options without a listbox, disabled reasons title-only, removal announces nothing. `/impeccable audit`
4. [P2] First run leads with dead controls and an empty black ring. Fix: collapse the bar until the queue has an entry; reveal animation group at 2; ritual steps inside the empty ring. `/impeccable onboard`
5. [P2] Mobile: 9 px horizontal overflow (stage + readout), readout clipped, bar is ~30% of a phone viewport. Phones undecided in PRODUCT.md. `/impeccable adapt`
